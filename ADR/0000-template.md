# ADR 0000 -- Template

> Architecture Decision Record. The discipline's permanent record of significant decisions.

## Status

Proposed | Accepted | Rejected | Superseded by ADR-NNNN

## Date

YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What is the situation that requires a decision? What forces are at play (technical, organisational, social, financial)? What evidence shows the situation is real?

## Decision

What did the discipline decide? State the decision in one sentence, then expand.

## Consequences

What becomes easier? What becomes harder? What does the discipline commit to as a result of this decision? What trade-offs are accepted?

## Alternatives considered

What other approaches were considered, and why are they not as good as the chosen approach?

## Migration path

If the decision is breaking, how do existing implementations, documents, or processes migrate to the new state? If the decision is additive, this section can be brief.

## Notes

Any other context that does not fit elsewhere.
